# One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

Entry for the make.fun bounty
`u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb` — write a single
sentence, at most 200 characters, that explains the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800, using only words
and concepts that existed in 1800.

## The sentence

> Imagine a great hall holding more letters, books, and songs than any king owns, where your word is heard at once by any soul anywhere, and their answer returns before your breath ends.

184 characters, one sentence.

## Why this picture

A traveler from 1800 knows great halls, kings, letters, books, and songs —
so the sentence builds only from things they already understand:

- **The great hall** is the internet itself: one place holding more writing
  and song than any king could own — the whole shared store of what people
  have written and made.
- **Your word is heard at once by any soul anywhere** — anyone can speak
  and be heard across the world in the same moment, which is what the
  internet does for messages.
- **Their answer returns before your breath ends** — it talks back just as
  fast, so the traveler grasps that it is a living exchange, not a shelf
  of dead books.

No word in the sentence was coined after 1800: no internet, computer,
website, email, phone, digital, or online — just a hall, letters, books,
songs, words, souls, and breath.

## Running it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints exactly the sentence above (one line). Under the make.fun program/v1
harness it also writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. `entry.txt`
contains the same sentence byte-for-byte.
